# Phase 2 report — Plan B (social roles) and Plan C (voluntary payoff sharing)

Generated by `scripts/34_phase2_report.py` (2026-10-20). Pre-registered criteria: AGENT_SPEC §5. Scripts: `30_phase2_build.py`, `31_phase2_planB.py`, `32_phase2_planC.py`, `33_phase2_cnn.py`.

## 1. Execution environment

CPU only for the linear analyses (minutes); the CNN variant (10 folds × 3 seeds) took ≈ 1 GPU-minute on the RTX 5080. Total Phase 2 compute ≪ the 20 GPU-h budget.

## 2. Data

- Group participants (NP/VP/FP): **477** in 103 groups; VP: **165**.
- Plan B input = own movement in the first 3 min (1 Hz table starts at 20 s → t ∈ [20, 180), 160 s): rotation statistics (mean/SD/P90 |Δθ|, zero and large-turn fractions, lag-1 autocorrelation, direction-flip rate), no-movement fraction, speed statistics from the 10 Hz positions, distance-to-resource statistics, tracking fraction and number of state switches. **No visible-other features.** The first-3-min score (40 − d points per second inside 40 m) is the pre-registered control.
- Plan B targets over t ≥ 180 s: mean in-degree, mean out-degree, in − out, number of own discoveries (205 of 477 participants have ≥ 1), discoverer in-degree gain (10 s after − 10 s before; n = 205). Discovery events reconstructed from the 10 Hz flags: 1,069 events whose discoverer is an analysed participant (the paper's events table has 1,090; the difference are events discovered by excluded fifth members of 4-person groups).
- Plan C targets (VP): sharing fraction (all / t ≥ 180 / while tracking / while searching), P(sharing within 10 s after a resource entry; median 13 entries per participant), P(sharing in the 10 s after an own discovery; n = 88).
- Split: `split_v1.json` (group-wise 10-fold); standardisation, PCA and ridge fitted on training participants of each fold.

## 3. Deviations from defaults

- **Linear z** (allowed by §4.3 after Phase 1): z = out-of-fold PCA-4 of the first-3-min own-movement statistics (unsupervised, so it carries no target information), decoded by ridge with the cell one-hot. Components aligned across folds to the all-sample PCA. The Phase 1 CNN encoder structure was also re-learned on the first-3-min sequences (§6, secondary).
- Variance decomposition uses the all-sample PCA scores (identical axes for all participants; no target leakage).
- **Plan C**: the pre-registered LOPO + Pearson r was dominated by the held-out-mean anti-correlation artefact (weak predictors gave r ≈ −1); replaced by the fixed group-wise 10-fold split, ridge on logit-transformed sharing (median sharing 0.06, max 0.98), and Spearman ρ with participant-bootstrap 95 % CI as the primary effect size.
- Mixed model for the paired test uses a **group** random intercept (participants are nested in groups).

## 5. Pass/fail (pre-registered §5.1: z beats the first-3-min-score baseline by Δr ≥ 0.05 with p < 0.01)

Decision of 2026-10-20 (human; questions.md): the criterion is written in Δr, so the p-value is computed **on Δr itself** with a group-level (cluster) bootstrap, per target; the squared-error mixed model (the Phase 1 test) is reported alongside. Cluster bootstrap (3,000 resamples of whole groups):

- **In-degree: PASS.** z alone Δr = 0.114 [cluster CI [0.026, 0.204]], p = 0.007.
- **Out-degree: FAIL.** z alone Δr = 0.079 [[-0.025, 0.174]], p = 0.14.
- **In − out: FAIL at p < 0.01** (Δr = 0.117, p = 0.039).
- **Verdict B-H1: PASS for in-degree, FAIL for out-degree.** Supplementary readings: the incremental test (z + score vs score) passes for in-degree and in − out (Δr 0.16, p < 0.002); the squared-error mixed model gives p = 0.052 for z alone (in-degree) — the discrepancy with the Δr test is not clustering but the quantity tested: ridge predictions are strongly shrunk (prediction SD = 17–33 % of the target SD), so z improves the *ranking* of participants clearly while improving squared error only marginally. Effect size summary: early own-movement style predicts later roles with r ≈ 0.25–0.33 (vs 0.1–0.17 for early score), ≈ 10 % of role variance beyond early performance.
- The in/out asymmetry is consistent with the decomposition: being looked at (in-degree) depends on one's own movement, looking at others (out-degree) depends more on the others' placement.
- **B-H2 (FP > NP): not supported.** z predicts in-degree about equally in NP, VP and FP (r 0.18 / 0.27 / 0.18); payoff information does not widen the expression of these individual differences in roles.
- **Decomposition:** own z explains ≈ 9–11 % of in/out-degree variance (LRT p < 0.001), the **others' mean z adds another ≈ 5–8 % (p < 0.001)** — one's visibility role depends on how the other members move — while the within-group SD of z adds nothing (p = 0.2–0.9), and the group random intercept is small (ICC ≤ 0.12 for in-degree, 0 for out-degree). Roles are therefore individual + "who you are with" (additively), not a composition effect — the same pattern as the pre-Phase-3 diagnostics.
- Discovery targets: the number of discoveries is explained by the speed cell (R² 0.33; discoveries are a fast-resource phenomenon) and z adds nothing; the discoverer in-degree gain (n = 205) is unpredictable from any source.

## 8. Anomalies / unresolved

- LOPO + Pearson artefact in Plan C (see §3); the 10-fold version is reported. Negative out-of-fold r/ρ values in the tables (e.g. sharing while tracking) mean "no signal" (predictions anti-correlate with the held-out fold mean), not a real negative relation.
- MixedLM in statsmodels hits the variance boundary (group variance → 0) for out-degree; those models were fitted as OLS (identical likelihood at zero group variance), and the paired-test mixed models that failed to converge fell back to the mean difference.
- Mixed-model p-values and bootstrap p-values disagree for in-degree (0.05 vs 0.008) — the clustering correction matters; both are reported.

## 9. Unverified

- Whether the first-3-min window contains social influence through *movement* (others are not in the features, but one's own trajectory in a group already reflects them): a cleaner individual-only z would need the Alone condition, which has no roles. Not testable here.
