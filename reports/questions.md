# Questions / decisions for the human (Collective-ID)

Format: date — phase — question — interim decision taken (so work could continue).

- 2026-09-24 — P0 — **4-person groups.** The analysed sample has 103 groups: 65 of size 5 and 38 of size 4 (one member excluded for data quality, as in the paper). The plan assumed ≈95 groups of 5. For Phase 3 leave-one-group-out simulation, should 4-person groups be (a) simulated with 4 agents, (b) simulated with 5 agents using the excluded member's z (their 10 Hz trace exists in processed_traces.csv but they failed validity checks), or (c) dropped? *Interim:* keep them and simulate 4 agents; report results with/without them.
- 2026-09-24 — P0 — **brms sampling budget.** Paper: 4 chains × (2000 warmup + 3000 sampling) with 100 threads. Here: 4 chains × (1000 warmup + 1500 sampling), 5 threads each (24-core machine; ≈2–3 h per model). Posterior means of random effects are the deliverable; is this acceptable or should the paper's exact budget be run overnight? *Interim:* reduced budget; convergence (R̂, ESS, divergences) reported.
- 2026-09-24 — P0 — **Distance weight has no individual random effect in the paper's model.** The paper's tracking model has participant-level random slopes for Detector, SocPos, SocMov and the two quality interactions (plus intercept), but *not* for DistFromRes. AGENT_SPEC §4.1 lists a "d weight" among the teacher targets. *Interim:* Phase 0 reproduces the paper's model exactly; Phase 1 teacher = the paper's 6 random effects (intercept + 5 slopes). A variant with a DistFromRes random slope can be added if requested.
- 2026-09-24 — P0 — **Normalisation constants.** The paper normalises SocPosInFOV and SocMovDir by the sample-wide max |angle| (≈45.75° and ≈180°, i.e. geometric constants). Spec §2.3 asks for fold-internal normalisation; since these maxima are fixed by geometry (FOV half-width, ±180°) no participant information leaks. *Interim:* use the geometric constants (45.75, 180) everywhere and note the (negligible) difference from the paper.
- 2026-09-24 — P0/P1 — **Provisional teacher.** The full brms tracking fit (277k rows, 621 participants) had not finished warm-up after 45 min on 20 cores (paper: ~3 days on 64 cores for all fits). To keep Phase 1 moving, the same model (features, fixed-effect structure with constant(0) terms, RE structure, truncated-Exponential likelihood, priors) was fitted by GPU empirical-Bayes / penalised ML with a Laplace marginal-likelihood correction (`scripts/05_teacher_eb.py`, 1.6 min). Deviation: diagonal RE covariance (brms: LKJ full). Phase 1 runs first on this teacher (suffix `_eb`); when brms finishes, the pipeline is re-run on the brms teacher and the teacher–teacher correlation per coefficient is reported. Is the EB teacher acceptable as the final teacher if the two agree (r > 0.9)? *Interim:* brms remains authoritative; EB is provisional.
- 2026-09-24 — P1 — **Participants without a search phase.** 53 of 621 participants (all slow resource: FP 27, A 10, NP 8, VP 8) have fewer than 10 s of searching (they never lost the resource after the first 20 s). They have no source sequence for the search→tracking transfer and are excluded from Phase 1 (n = 568). For Phase 3 their z must come from the tracking phase (or the search-phase z must be imputed). This favours the tracking-phase z default for Phase 3. *Interim:* excluded in Phase 1; reported.
- 2026-09-24 — P0 — **Full brms fit abandoned (time).** The paper-code brms tracking model (277k rows, 621 participants, 4 chains × 2500 iterations, 5 threads/chain on 24 cores) reached warm-up iteration 200 of chain 1 only after 4 h 45 min (started 10:23, iteration 200 at 15:08). Projected completion: > 1 day for this model alone; the search and four split-half fits would add several more days. The fit was stopped at 15:10. Instead: (i) the GPU empirical-Bayes fit of the same model is the teacher (all six fits take ≈ 10 min); (ii) a brms fit on a 20 % participant subsample (4 × 1500 iterations) quantifies EB–brms agreement of the random effects on the same participants. Should the full brms fits be run over a weekend anyway (e.g. with initial values from the EB fit to shorten warm-up)? *Interim:* EB teacher; subsample brms as validation.
- 2026-09-24 — P1 — **Pre-registered baseline is uninformative.** A-H1's Δr ≥ 0.10 is defined against the condition × speed mean of the teacher random effects, but random effects are deviations from condition × speed fixed effects, so cell means are ≈ 0 and the constant baseline has r ≤ 0 (leave-fold-out artefact gives r ≈ −0.4). Δr vs cell mean is therefore trivially large (0.47) while the encoder's actual r averages 0.09 and it loses to the linear ridge baseline. Proposal for Phases 2–3: define Δr against r = 0 (constant prediction) and against the linear baseline; keep the mixed-model p-value. The original criterion was applied as written for the Phase 1 verdict (FAIL because ridge wins).
- 2026-09-24 — P0/P1 — **EB teacher validated.** brms (paper code) on a 20 % participant subsample (121 participants, 4 × 1500 iterations, R̂ 1.015, ESS ≥ 528, 0 divergences, 98 min) vs the EB fit: per-participant random effects correlate r = 0.99–0.998 (intercept, detector) and 0.90–0.94 (social weights and quality interactions) on the same participants (`results/phase0/teacher_eb_vs_brms.csv`). Interim decision stands: EB teacher for Phases 1–3 unless the human requests the multi-day brms run.
- 2026-10-02 — pre-P3 — **Diagnostics before Phase 3** (`reports/diagnostics_pre_phase3.md`). (ii) In the observed data, first-half group-mean traits predict second-half group outcome (ΔR² 0.16, perm p < 0.001), the within-group SD of the reliable traits adds ΔR² 0.02 (perm p 0.35). Decisions requested: (a) run Phase 3 in reduced form (D-1 linear mapping, no D-2) as recommended, in full, or stop after Phase 2? (b) add a **mean-preserving** z-shuffle control, because the pre-registered shuffle (control 4) changes the group mean and will make D-H2 pass trivially? *Interim:* nothing started; awaiting the decision.
