## Discrepancies / notes

- Groups: 103 (65 of size 5, 38 of size 4) vs the plan's ≈95 estimate ((621−144)/5 = 95.4 assumes all groups of 5). 4-person groups are kept as in the paper; the leave-one-group-out design in Phase 3 must handle n=4 groups (simulate 4 agents, or 5 with the excluded member — decision logged in questions.md).
- Resource discovery events: paper text says 'all searching for at least 5 s ... stayed for at least 5 s'; code (localize_events) uses 20 frames = 2 s for both, with a 2 s offset removed in R. Measured definition (code) is used here.
- Frame-rate exclusion (paper: >5% below 10 Hz) is implemented as: A → resource duplicate frames <5%; groups → time_in_experiment <15.75 min. No per-frame frame-rate variable exists; duplicates_percentage (A) and time_in_experiment (groups) are the available nuisance proxies.
