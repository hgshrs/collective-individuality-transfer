## 2. Data

- Source: search-phase 1 s sequences (features: |Δθ|, signed Δθ, θ_pos, θ_mov, binary social quality, #visible, normalised distance; Alone participants have zeros in social features). Target: the paper's tracking-model participant random effects (intercept, Detector, θ_pos, θ_mov, θ_pos×quality, θ_mov×quality) from `runs/eb/tracking_all_ranef.csv` (EB fit; brms full fit abandoned for time — see phase0_report §4.4 and questions.md).
- Split: `data/processed/split_v1.json` (10 folds, stratified by condition × speed, whole groups in one fold). Standardisation of inputs and of targets uses training participants of the fold only.
- **Excluded from Phase 1: 53 participants with < 10 s of searching** (all slow resource: FP 27, A 10, NP 8, VP 8; they never lost the resource), leaving n = 568. They have no source sequence; see questions.md.
- Teacher used for this report: `EB (GPU empirical-Bayes fit of the paper model, provisional)`. Split-half reliability of the teacher random effects (noise ceiling) is in phase0_report §4.5.

## 3. Deviations from defaults

- Target includes the participant intercept (paper RE structure) and excludes a distance weight (no such random slope in the paper's model) — see questions.md.
- Early stopping uses a 10 % stratified validation subset of the training participants (spec: 'fold 内 early stop').
- Baseline 'search-phase score' (§4.2-4) implemented as search-time-only ridge (search phase has zero score by definition).

## 5. Pass/fail (pre-registered §4.3)

- A-H1: **FAIL** (criterion: mean Δr ≥ 0.10 vs condition×speed mean, mixed-model p < 0.01, and beats ridge). Observed: Δr_cond = 0.469, Δr_ridge = -0.062.
- A-H2: **PASS** (negative performance–distance slope, p < 0.001, z distance beats summary-statistic distance).
- Per §4.3: the non-linear encoder lost to the linear ridge baseline on every reliably-measured coefficient → **'非線形の z は不要'**; the linear z (`results/phase1/z_linear_pls4_ridge_eb.csv`, PLS scores of search-phase summary statistics) is the recommended substitute for a search-phase z. Since 53 participants have no search phase and social weights have a low noise ceiling, Phase 3 should default to a **tracking-phase-derived z** (spec default), with the linear search-phase z as a secondary analysis.

## 6. Anomalies / failures

- The full brms tracking fit (paper code) was stopped after 4 h 45 min at warm-up iteration 200/2500 (projected > 1 day per model); teacher = GPU empirical-Bayes fit of the identical model (diagonal RE covariance). A 20 % subsample brms fit is running to quantify EB–brms agreement (§7).
- Pipeline script bug (extra `--tag` flag) aborted the simulated-agent and ICC steps once; both were re-run manually with identical arguments (results above).
- The `_tuned` variant was run with one seed, so its mixed-model p-values are degenerate (NaN) — its Δr and bootstrap CIs are valid.
- The CNN encoder overfits within ~10 epochs (training loss → 0.3 while validation never improves beyond epoch ≈ 5); stronger regularisation did not help. With 450 training participants and low-reliability targets, a sequence encoder has no advantage over hand-crafted summary statistics.
- A-H2 caveat: because the decoder is a smooth function of z, prediction error necessarily grows with ‖z_k − z_l‖; the informative part of the test is that z distance explains error better than the raw summary-statistic distance (it does, in the joint model), not the slope itself.

## 7. Unverified

- Agreement between the EB teacher and the paper's brms estimator is only checked on a 10 % / 20 % participant subsample (`results/phase0/teacher_eb_vs_brms.csv`; pending until the subsample fit finishes). Fixed effects of the EB fit reproduce the paper's Fig 4 orderings.
- Results are on 568 participants (search-phase available); generalisation to the 53 never-searching participants is untested.
